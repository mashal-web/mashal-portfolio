COURSE NOTES / CV-110L

Applied Physics & Electro Mechanical Fundamental

A structured overview of the course information sheet and lab plan supplied for Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology, Civil Engineering Department.

SOURCE SESSION: FALL 2024 · 1 CREDIT HOUR · 1ST SEMESTER

COURSE DETAILS

Course at a glance

Course code: CV-110L

Prerequisites: Nil

Mode of teaching: Synchronous

Instructor listed in source: Syed Noman Mahmood

The stated objective is to practise electrical-circuit fundamentals using components such as resistors and equipment such as multimeters and power supplies, including series and parallel resistor combinations and circuit theorems.

COURSE OUTLINE

Core topics

01

Electrical basics

Lab equipment familiarization, resistor colour code, resistance measurement, multimeter use, and Ohm’s law.

02

Circuit networks

Series and parallel circuits, voltage-divider circuits, current measurement, and related circuit principles.

03

Machines

Transformer action, transformation ratio, winding resistance, efficiency, voltage regulation, and DC-machine observations.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

Course learning outcomes

CLO 1 · PLO 4

Perform and measure

Follow experimental instructions and measure resistance, voltage, and current in resistive networks, and observe transformer and DC-motor characteristics. The sheet maps this to Problem Analysis and Bloom’s level P3 (Guided Response).

CLO 2 · PLO 10

Explain principles

Explain basic electrical engineering principles, transformers, and DC motors. The sheet maps this to Communication and Bloom’s level A3 (Valuing).

CEA

Subject project

The sheet describes a subject project for groups of 2–4 students, based on CLO 1 and requiring basic electrical fundamentals and diverse resources.

LAB PLAN

Practical sequence

WEEK 01–06

Basic measurements and circuits

Resistor colour code and resistance (manual pp. 3–8); multimeter, resistance and voltage (pp. 9–11); Ohm’s law (pp. 12–16); series circuits (pp. 17–25); unloaded voltage-divider circuits (pp. 26–34); current in parallel circuits (pp. 35–39).

WEEK 07–11

Transformer experiments

Week 7 is open-ended and week 8 is midterm. Transformer transformation ratio and winding resistance (pp. 40–45); autotransformer ratio (pp. 46–50); transformer efficiency and voltage regulation at different loads (pp. 51–53).

WEEK 12–16

DC machines and examination

DC-machine nameplate data (pp. 54–58); voltage regulation of a self-excited DC generator (pp. 59–60); speed regulation (pp. 61–65); week 15 open-ended; week 16 lab examination.

The source’s broader course breakdown also lists physics topics such as matter and semiconductors, electromagnetic induction, vectors and fields, mechanics, waves, modern physics, transducers, heat transfer, thermodynamics, engines, and HVAC.
